# Welcome to COW Shed

COW Shed is a smart contract infrastructure that enables secure, permissioned hooks for CoW Protocol orders through deterministic proxy deployment and EIP-712 signatures.

  <Card title="Deterministic Deployment" icon="location-dot" href="/architecture">

  <Card title="Multi-Wallet Support" icon="wallet" href="/concepts/signatures">

## What Can You Build?

COW Shed enables powerful use cases by allowing permissioned actions during order execution:

- **Just-in-Time Liquidity**: Borrow assets right before a swap and repay after
- **Cross-Chain Bridging**: Automatically bridge swapped tokens to another chain
- **Claim and Swap**: Claim vested tokens and immediately swap them
- **Collateral Management**: Add collateral or repay debt positions atomically with swaps

## Multi-Chain Deployment

COW Shed is deployed at consistent addresses across multiple EVM chains including Ethereum, Gnosis Chain, Polygon, Arbitrum, Base, and more.
